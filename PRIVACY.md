# Privacy policy – Jakub Kosnar Publisher

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Jakub Kosnar Publisher is a personal tool run by Jakub Kosnar, a photographer in Prague, Czech Republic. It posts Jakub's own photographs to his own Pinterest and Instagram accounts.

- **Users:** only Jakub. There are no other users and no sign-up.
- **Data accessed:** Jakub's own Pinterest boards and pins, and the photos and captions he prepares. The tool does not access anyone else's account or data.
- **Storage:** access tokens are kept encrypted in a self-hosted automation tool on Jakub's computer. Photos are uploaded to this repository only so the platforms can fetch them, and are removed after posting.
- **Sharing:** no data is sold or shared with anyone other than Pinterest and Meta, which receive the posts.
- **Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/KevinKosnar/photo-posts-media/issues
